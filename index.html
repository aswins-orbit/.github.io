02 — Research

Current Research

An ongoing M.Tech research project at IIT Madras exploring advanced two-phase thermal transport.

IIT MADRAS • M.TECH THERMAL ENGINEERING

Microchannel Flow Boiling: Experiments & Simulation

My current research investigates flow boiling in microchannels using experimental and computational approaches. The work is motivated by the need for compact and high-performance thermal management technologies.

Areas of interest include boiling heat transfer, two-phase flow behaviour, pressure drop, critical heat flux and thermal performance of micro-scale cooling systems.

Guide: Prof. Aravind Pattamatta
